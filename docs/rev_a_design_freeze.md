## Cell monitor configuration

The complete **39-entry RAM-only** selection is [bq76942_rev_a.json](../firmware/config/bq76942_rev_a.json), audited by [offline checker](../tools/check_cell_profile.py). It is not MCU firmware and has not been written to hardware. Use SPI CRC;REG1=3.3 V;9S mask0x02FF (cells1–8+10);COV code50=>2.530 V,CUV24=>1.2144 V;delay code1=>about10 ms;hysteresis101.2 mV. Enable COV/CUV and monitor internal OT80°C;all main current protections remain on independent external hardware because SRP/SRN are grounded.

DCHG/DDSG config0xA6 means **active-low fault,healthy-high permission**,REG1 high drive and pulldown. It is not active-high fault. CHG protection A0x18 and DSG protection A0xE4 follow TI's fast-output mapping guidance;other masks use internal OT. FET_CTRL_EN remains set for logical outputs,charge pump disabled and CP1 tied BAT per the low-side application note;FET_INIT_OFF forces initial permission off. Readback and cell-simulator fault tests precede MON_CONFIG_VALID/ARM. Disable SLEEP for predictable monitoring;repeat RAM provisioning after every reset. No OTP programming,blind FET_ENABLE toggle or FET test command is permitted by this profile.

Open-wire check interval1 s;test every tap disconnect at commissioning and log actual latency. Alarm pin active-low/open-drain with external REG1 pullup. TS pins are unused;external bank NTC hardware is the real bank thermal protection. PF/chemical-fuse engine is not enabled in this prototype profile;the existing external latched shutdown remains mandatory. Configuration mismatch,CRC failure,monitor power loss or failed tap test inhibits operation.

Host-only internal switched-passive balancing:one cell at a time,1 s maximum command,converter disabled,all readings valid,bank0–40°C,cells>=2.3 V,start difference20 mV,stop10 mV. Ten source-side1kΩ1W resistors physically at electrodes plus ten onboard1kΩ1W input resistors protect the tap harness. A single source resistor at24.3 V dissipates at most0.591 W in the simplified short model. This arrangement allows only slow trim (~0.6 mA ignoring switch resistance),not correction during full40 W charge. Large imbalance stops charge and requires separate low-current commissioning;empty-bank startup is not the normal mode. Measurement/open-wire/balancing settling through2kΩ per tap must be tested;do not infer protection from bank voltage alone.

Primary sources: [BQ76942 TRM](https://www.ti.com/lit/ug/sluuby1b/sluuby1b.pdf), [low-side output configuration](https://www.ti.com/lit/an/sluaa84a/sluaa84a.pdf), [TI fast-output mapping FAQ](https://e2e.ti.com/support/power-management-group/power-management/f/power-management-forum/1237508/faq-bq76952-do-the-dchg-and-ddsg-signals-on-the-bq769x2-follow-the-chg-and-dsg-fet-driver-pin-states).

## External bench assembly / power / fuses

Four Panasonic AEV14012 contactors:BUS_ISO,CAP_ISO,BYP_BUS,BYP_CAP. Their logical contact-symbol COM/NO/COIL pins are assembly terminals,not manufacturer's housing numbering. Observe manufacturer's polarity and tightening instructions. Product is NRND;existing stock only,approximately1.6 kg total contactor mass. This selects a bench prototype assembly,not a robot-ready mechanical design. Their minimum rated load1 A/12 V prevents relying on them for0.26 A precharge;two AQZ202G AC/DC PhotoMOS devices handle that path. AQZ physical1=LED−,2=LED+,3/4=AC/DC load;external module wiring follows these numbers. LED150Ω from3.3 V with BC847B low-side driver,base1.5kΩ and pulldown;input current/turn-off must be measured.

MEAN WELL DDR-60L-12 supplies actuator12 V on a separate bus branch after the referee measurement/cutoff boundary. Physical terminals1/2=−Vo,3/4=+Vo,5=+Vin,6=−Vin;output− is intentionally bonded systemGND. At22–26 V input apply manufacturer's low-input output derating;reserve24 W output,do not use full60 W nameplate. Four coil maximum planning current1.553 A;gate-driver LM5164 rail does not carry this load. Include input inrush (datasheet20 A at48 V) in source and5 A fuse tests;its duration atactual24 V is unknown. Both actuator under/overvoltage windows inhibit hardware permits (nominal~11.0/13.64 V). Switching supplies can fail,so manual upstream disconnect remains required.

Use Littelfuse MINI997 58 V fuses:0997010.WXN10 A bus,0997015.WXN15 A bank,0997005.WXN5 A actuator. Each in0FHM0002ZXJM58 V waterproof MINI holder;crimp/solder/harness temperatures and holder voltage/current rating checked before assembly. ATOF is32 V and the previous58 V ATOF label is superseded. MINI997 interrupt specification1000 A at58 V is a **component rating**,not assembled interruption proof. Initial qualification condition:measured prospective current<=500 A at<=26 V;use current-limited source before high-energy tests. Typical melting I²t93/270/25 A²s is not guaranteed maximum clearing I²t;do not claim coordination with semiconductor SOA until actual clear-time/let-through and cable withstand are known. Initial harness selection:short2.5 mm² copper power conductors,insulation>=100 V/105°C,documented crimps,with measured connection temperatures;wire product/order availability remains procurement review rather than fabricated evidence.

SMBJ18CA bidirectional suppression across each contactor coil replaces the slow freewheel diode.12 V plus rated clamp29.2 V suggests41.2 V drain stress in that simplified pulse model;measure actual coil current,release and VDS. Require contact opening<=50 ms including suppressor in qualification;AEV datasheet30 ms release excludes protective components. Gate kill handles fast faults;contacts/fuses contain persistent failures. For shorted FET,local shoot-through,welded contact or dead auxiliary supply,verify both healthy isolation contacts andmanual source/bank disconnect;no firmware command or PWM OFF constitutes physical isolation.

Precharge/dump/service resistors:HS25 100R F mounted with thermal compound to a dedicated external aluminum plate at least250x120x3 mm (~600 cm² two faces);verify mounting surface and measured hotspot<80°C for prototype service.25 W requires proper heatsink mounting;maximum design dissipation here about6.83 W at26 V/R−1%,not a pulse-rating certification. Bodies/wiring guarded against touch. Service tool includes its own meter,100Ω HS25 and Blue Sea6006 maintained disconnect;it is independent of MCU/bus auxiliary power. Source and bank manual disconnects also use6006 (48 VDC device,25 A switching listing;300 A continuous listing is not an interrupt rating). Never manually break prospective fault current;fuse/source containment must first remove it. The external schematic shows all three manual paths. Select Alpha Wire5859 PTFE14AWG red/black for short bench power wiring (replaces generic2.5mm² proposal above;14AWG≈2.08mm²),with covered bolted lugs and measured<60°C joints. Manufacturer wire specification lists600 VRMS/200°C but this does not establish a bundled harness current rating. Its mechanical terminations and switching/interrupt behavior must be inspected/tested before use.
